Copy to reader.md (gitignored) and describe yourself in one sentence. It is
dropped into the explainer's system prompt, so the answers are pitched at you
rather than at a generic reader.

Examples:

  a final-year computer science student who has finished a data structures
  course and has written no production code yet.

  a staff backend engineer who has shipped distributed systems for eight years
  and is evaluating whether to put an LLM in a product.
